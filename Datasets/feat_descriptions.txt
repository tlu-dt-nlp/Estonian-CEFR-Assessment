Linguistic features extracted from the texts


Surface features:

"word_count" - number of words
"sent_count" - number of sentences
"word_length" - mean word length in characters
"sent_length" - mean sentence length in words
"LIX" - LIX complexity index
"SMOG" - SMOG complexity index
"FK" - Flesch-Kincaid Reading Ease index
"syllable_count" - number of syllables 
"polysyllables" - number of words with three of more syllables


Lexical features:

"lemma_count" - number of unique words (lemmas)
"TTR" - type-token ratio (lemmas are considered as types)
"RTTR" - root type-token ratio (Guiraud index)
"Uber" - Uber index of lexical diversity
"Maas" - Maas index of lexical diversity
"MTLD" - Measure of Textual Lexical Diversity
"CVV" - corrected verb variation
"A_TTR" - adjective TTR
"D_TTR" - adverb TTR
"I_TTR" - interjection TTR
"J_TTR" - conjunction TTR
"K_TTR" - adposition TTR
"N_TTR" - numeral TTR
"P_TTR" - pronoun TTR
"S_TTR" - noun TTR
"V_TTR" - verb TTR

"S_abstr" - average noun abstractness on the scale 1-3
"rare_5000" - ratio of vocabulary not among 5,000 most frequent Estonian words
"rare_4000" - ratio of vocabulary not among 4,000 most frequent Estonian words
"rare_3000" - ratio of vocabulary not among 3,000 most frequent Estonian words
"rare_2000" - ratio of vocabulary not among 2,000 most frequent Estonian words
"rare_1000" - ratio of vocabulary not among 1,000 most frequent Estonian words
"LD" - lexical density (the proportion of lexical words)

"avg_error_word_ratio" - average ratio of grammar-corrected words in a sentence
